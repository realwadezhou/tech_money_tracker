# Bill Gates (p0017)

## Review approach

The Gates Foundation biography identifies Microsoft, the foundation and Breakthrough Energy; Microsoft's annual report establishes WILLIAM H. GATES III. Preserve III as discriminating evidence, including when it appears before the comma. Accept Bill/William/H/III variants only with these specific affiliations and compatible leadership/philanthropy context. William Gates at HashLynx, real-estate firms, a union or a fire department is not silently joined. Firefighter, union-laborer, professor, construction-superintendent and named real-estate-broker contexts are rejected for substantive professional conflicts. Other middle initials/generational suffixes block the initial merge. Microsoft chairman labels are explicitly historical-role evidence rather than current employment.

Examined 111 candidate evidence groups containing 487 source records. Accepted 95 record links. Counts are not distinct gifts or dollar totals.
