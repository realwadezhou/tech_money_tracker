The recipient-based Democratic percentage divides dollars to Democratic-leaning committees by dollars to Democratic- plus Republican-leaning committees.

Pct Classified measures the share of company-linked dollars going to Democratic- or Republican-leaning recipient committees. Pct Dem by Donor instead measures dollars from Democratic-leaning donors divided by dollars from Democratic- plus Republican-leaning donors; it is not a share of people or all giving. Donor lean uses all included contributions under the reported name, including unmatched employers. Mixed and Unknown groups are excluded from each Democratic percentage.
