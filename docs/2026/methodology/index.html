Methodology

How the numbers on this site are built: what's counted, what isn't, and what each column label means. New to campaign finance terms? Start with Campaign Finance 101.

Where the data comes from

This site uses Federal Election Commission bulk files: individual contributions, candidate master records, committee master records, candidate-committee links, and committee transactions including independent expenditures. FEC committee history also confirms former campaign accounts that converted to PACs. This site groups and aggregates those records.

The FEC individual-contributions file includes some contributions from partnerships, organizations, and other nonindividual sources. Included transaction types, rather than an individual-only entity filter, define the contribution pool. Employer matching does not verify that a contributor is an employee or that a company directed the contribution.

How donors are matched to tech companies

Itemized contribution records contain donor details, including an employer when reported. The employer is free text, so "Google," "Google LLC," "google inc," "goog," and "alphabet" all appear as different strings in the raw data. Matching tech donors means keeping a lookup that maps these messy strings to a clean canonical company name, built and maintained by hand. Contributions get counted toward a company only when the employer string matches an entry in that lookup.

What is counted

  • Included itemized contribution records to federal committees, with refund transaction types subtracted.
  • Contributions are attributed to a tech company when the donor's employer string maps to a tracked company in the hand-built lookup.
  • Candidate-level account receipts include principal and authorized campaign accounts plus FEC-confirmed converted former campaign accounts linked to that election cycle. Current and former account amounts are exported separately and sum to the combined account total. Historical account totals can include post-conversion PAC activity; they must not be quoted as direct campaign contributions without further review.
  • Other joint fundraising, leadership PAC, and unauthorized affiliates are excluded from candidate receipt totals. The committee master and FEC conversion history resolve candidate links; links that remain ambiguous are excluded. Historical account attribution does not determine committee party lean, which is classified separately.
  • When an account changes candidates, the current committee-master association assigns its selected cycle receipts to one candidate. The pipeline does not reconstruct ownership on each receipt date. Zero assigned receipts for a former candidate are not evidence of zero historical campaign giving.
  • Independent expenditures (IE Support / IE Oppose) come from support and opposition records in the FEC's committee-transaction file and are reported separately from account receipts. These totals cover all included reporting committees, not just committees receiving tech contributions.
  • Exported tech-funded IE figures cover spending by committees with any positive net tech receipts. They do not identify which donor's dollars paid for a particular expenditure.
  • Party lean for non-party committees (PACs, Super PACs) is inferred from their candidate-facing spending when the committee itself has no direct party affiliation.

What isn't counted

  • Contributions reported only as unitemized totals — these have no donor names or employers to match.
  • Donors whose employer string doesn't match a tracked tech company — including "Self-employed," odd abbreviations, blanks, and typos that haven't been mapped yet.
  • Companies not on the curated tracked-company list.
  • Company PAC giving is not separately attributed to its connected company in tech-linked contribution totals.
  • Outbound spending by committees (ads, operations, transfers) beyond the IE views mentioned above.

Current limits

  • Cycles currently covered: 2024 and 2026.
  • A committee's "Tech Share" uses net itemized contributions in the FEC individual-contributions file — not all money received. It is omitted when refunds or included nonzero memo attributions prevent a meaningful denominator.
  • Partnership contributions can include a parent receipt and memo records attributing that receipt to partners. These records can describe the same money. We retain attribution records for employer analysis, but do not display an all-record receipt total or calculate Tech Share for groups with unresolved memo attributions. The downloadable selected_record_net_total preserves the raw selected-record sum; nonmemo_receipt_net_total is a diagnostic, not a fully reconciled substitute. has_unreconciled_memo_attributions and memo_receipt_record_count identify affected groups.
  • The exported tech-dominated committee count requires a valid Tech Share strictly above 50%; an exact 50% or an undefined share does not qualify.
  • Refunds are matched to companies using their own reported employer field. A refund with a blank or unmatched employer reduces the overall contribution pool but cannot reduce a tech-company total through name matching alone. Tech-linked totals therefore mean net employer-matched records, not a fully reconciled donor ledger.
  • Donors are grouped by exact reported name, which can combine different people with the same name and split one person across name variants. Donor party classifications use all included records under that name; company and tech-giving totals use only employer-matched records.
  • District and Senate exports count donor/candidate pairs in tech_donor_candidate_pairs. The older tech_itemized_donors column in those aggregate files is a compatibility alias for the same sum, not distinct people across candidates. Candidate-level donor counts deduplicate reported names across that candidate's included accounts.
  • Candidate navigation requires the candidate master election year to equal the selected even-year cycle. Odd-year special elections and fundraising by candidates for other election years are omitted. State and district groups are geographic summaries, not verified ballots or separate primary/general/special contests.
  • Weekly charts begin their display at 2025-01-01; totals shown include earlier rows present in the source files.
  • Per-candidate detail pages beyond national, state, Senate, and House-district views aren't built yet.

Column glossary

Plain-language meanings for every column label currently used on the site.

SectionThe site area or page family listed in a navigation table.
What It ShowsA short plain-language description of that page or export.
CompanyThe tracked tech company or firm tied to a donor-employer tag.
Candidate CommitteeA candidate-linked committee receiving contributions.
CommitteeA political committee, which may be a candidate committee, PAC, party committee, Super PAC, or another filer.
DonorA contributor name as grouped in the current dataset.
NameThe donor name shown on the major-donor table.
StateThe state-level Senate contest bucket or state navigation row.
DistrictA U.S. House district row.
JurisdictionA non-state jurisdiction such as DC or a territory.
CandidateAn individual candidate from the FEC candidate master records and candidate-committee link files.
PartyThe candidate or committee party label shown in the export.
LeanA simplified D, R, Mixed, or Unknown label based on where classified tech money flowed, not a race rating.
TypeThe committee type from FEC filings, such as candidate committee, PAC, or party committee.
BucketA broad committee category used on this site: candidate, party, PAC, outside spending, communication cost, or other.
SectorThe company-sector label from the tracked-company lookup.
TotalThe total dollars for that row's main entity in that specific table. On company, donor, and committee tables, this is the full amount shown for that entity.
Total ($)The donor name's net tech-matched giving in dollars.
Account ReceiptsThe net selected-record sum in the FEC individual-contributions file for principal and authorized campaign accounts plus FEC-confirmed former campaign accounts. Shown as Unreconciled when nonzero memo attributions could overlap parent receipts. Former accounts can include post-conversion PAC activity; outside spending is excluded.
Tech Account ReceiptsThe employer-matched subset of Account Receipts, including separately identified former campaign accounts.
Current-Account TechEmployer-matched receipts to linked accounts currently classified as principal or authorized campaign committees. This is an account classification, not a verified election-designation total.
Former-Account Tech / TotalEmployer-matched / all included itemized receipts to confirmed former campaign accounts. No conversion-date split is available; these amounts may include later PAC receipts.
Top D by Tech / Top R by TechThe Democratic / Republican candidate with the highest tech-account receipts in the geographic group. Not a declaration of nominees or election winners.
Tech ReceiptsThe portion of committee receipts carrying a tracked tech-employer tag.
Tech-Linked GivingThe site-wide sum of tech-matched contribution dollars in the current dataset.
Tech ShareThe share of net itemized contributions in the FEC individual-contributions file carrying tracked tech-employer tags. Omitted when refunds or retained nonzero memo attributions prevent a meaningful denominator. Selected-record dollar sums remain available in downloads for review.
Tech DonorsThe count of distinct reported donor-name strings in the tech-matched subset. These are not verified unique people.
DonorsThe count of distinct reported donor-name strings represented by that row. Name variants can split a person, and shared names can combine different people.
CommitteesThe number of committees represented or linked in that table row.
Linked CommitteesThe number of committees affiliated with a candidate, from the FEC candidate-committee link file. This includes committees whose receipts are excluded from candidate totals.
Committee NamesThe names of committees linked to that candidate.
Top CommitteeThe committee receiving the largest share of money from that donor or company row.
Top Committee FundedThe committee that received the most money from that donor in the public export.
Company TagsThe tracked tech-company labels attached to a donor or committee through matched contributions.
Pct ClassifiedThe share of dollars that could be assigned a party direction under the site's classification rules.
Pct Dem by DonorThe share of dollars from Democratic-leaning donors among dollars from Democratic- and Republican-leaning donors in that company summary. This uses donor classifications and is not a share of donor counts.
$ to DemNet giving under that donor name to Democratic-leaning committees, including records with unmatched employers.
% to DemThe Democratic share of that donor's classified giving.
$ to RepNet giving under that donor name to Republican-leaning committees, including records with unmatched employers.
% to RepThe Republican share of that donor's classified giving.
IE SupportIndependent expenditures by outside committees reported as supporting the candidate. These are not direct receipts to the candidate committee.
IE OpposeIndependent expenditures by outside committees reported as opposing the candidate. These are not direct receipts to the candidate committee.
RowsThe number of contribution rows rolled into that summary entry.
House DistrictsThe number of House districts represented in that state or jurisdiction row.
Senate CandidatesThe number of Senate candidates represented in that state or jurisdiction row.
FileA downloadable export file.
JSONA JSON payload produced for the site.
CycleThe election cycle this page covers.
Latest Matched TransactionThe latest valid transaction date among tech-matched records in the current dataset. This is not the last download date or a statement that all filings through that date are included.
Tracked CompaniesThe number of companies and firms with matched contributions in this cycle.

Why the site looks like this

The design goal is plain HTML, minimal CSS, a warm document background, and JavaScript only where charts or table sorting and filtering require it.
