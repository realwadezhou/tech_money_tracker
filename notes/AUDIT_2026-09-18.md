## FEC findings and corrections

- **Special-account refunds:** `41Y`/`42Y` were treated as incoming receipts.
  Corrected their signs and included the missing corresponding special-account
  refund types. Across the source rows this reduces the 2024 overall record
  pool by $106,988 and 2026 by $630. The affected records have no matched
  employers, so this specific fix does not change tech numerators.
- **Campaign versus former-PAC accounts:** historical association does not
  supply a conversion-date cutoff. Added separate current campaign-account
  and former-account components, retaining legacy combined fields. Removed
  inference of an independent expenditure's candidate target from a former
  account ID alone. Candidate IDs explicitly reported in the source remain
  usable.
- **Visibility and counts:** refund-only and offsetting matched activity must
  not vanish from a candidate page while remaining in geographic aggregates.
  Added explicit donor–candidate-pair aliases for summed regional name counts.
  D/R leaders are labeled as highest amounts, not nominees.
- **Join integrity:** duplicate committee IDs can multiply every joined
  contribution; conflicting aliases can silently assign the wrong company.
  Both now fail for review. Identical alias repeats do not multiply amounts,
  and legitimate equal-value contribution records are preserved.
- **Source freshness:** a failed remote check is now unknown, not current.
  Installed release date is separate from the latest observed remote release,
  maximum matched transaction date, build time, and filing completeness.
- **API pagination:** itemized Schedule A/B requests now follow returned
  cursors, including resumed queries and null-date transitions. Repeated or
  missing cursors fail rather than silently repeat page-one totals.
- **Partnership attribution:** two verified $25 million partnership gifts at
  `C00916114` each have two $12.5 million partner memo attributions. The matched
  tech records credit partners once; an all-record sum repeats the underlying
  receipt when it includes the parents too. Affected Tech Share percentages
  and displayed all-record receipt totals are suppressed pending reconciliation,
  with diagnostic sums preserved in downloads. The broader committee had
  $62.5 million in tech attribution records and $125.1 million in selected
  records before this fix; the two partnership gifts are only part of it.
  This is not solved by discarding every memo row.
- **Repeated original contribution:** a later memo can repeat an original
  gift before recording redesignation or reattribution children. Ten
  source-reviewed exclusions remove $39,736 in repeated memos in 2024 and
  $15,500 in 2026, preserving originals and signed adjustments. The first
  examples were Lila Flores ($6,600, Palantir) and Emilie Choi ($3,300, Coinbase).
  The
  common loader matches cycle, source ID, and a full-record hash, with filing
  evidence rather than broad name/date/amount deduplication. Changed contents
  stop the build for review. A separate full-source dependency check also
  requires each retained original and its reviewed contents to remain present
  when the corresponding memo is excluded. The reference is
  `data/reference/transactions/reviewed_exclusions.json`.

The remaining type-`15E` memo exclusion is explicitly unresolved. Examples
include both destination evidence and money already represented upstream.
The $53,158 (2024) and $10,400 (2026) of matched memo exposure are not estimates
of missing contributions. The source-reviewed corrections do not establish
universal gift-level reconciliation across every filer and amendment.
Account handovers have a separate limitation: shared receipts follow the
committee master's candidate association, without a historical-date split.
The 2024 Biden/Harris account illustrates why a zero assigned to the former
candidate cannot be quoted as zero historical giving. The guide and candidate
pages now state this explicitly.

The exact-name/date/amount review was a bounded scan of mixed memo/nonmemo
tech groups, not a complete duplicate detector. All 29 flagged groups were
checked against official source filings: ten repeats were corrected, seven
correctly offsetting groups were retained, and twelve remain unresolved.
At `C00876383`, 12 same-filing
pairs differ only in transaction ID and memo flag. The paired memo records
total $18,944. The filing's itemized receipts agree with its nonmemo sum, but
there are no backreferences explaining which original gifts those memos
attribute. The available FEC request for additional information concerns
disbursements, not these receipts. These rows remain unresolved and retained;
the associated Tech Share is withheld. They must be investigated before using
that committee's employer-matched figure as a fully reconciled giving total.

Source precision also matters: one reviewed electronic filing contains
$3,436.02 while the corresponding bulk record contains $3,436. The exclusions
and resulting site changes use the actual bulk amounts, not invented cents.

## September 19 FEC attribution cleanup

Implemented a separate source-based workflow in `pipeline/fec/filings.py`,
`pipeline/fec/attribution.py`, `pipeline/fec/campaign_reports.py`, and
`scripts/build_candidate_attribution.py`. Reviewed manifests are tracked in
`data/reference/attribution/`; the plain-English developer guide explains how
to reproduce them and add another bounded case.

The source loader supports electronic F3/F3P/F3X reports in EFO 8.4/8.5. It
checks original-file fingerprints and complete amendment sequences, selects
whole replacement reports, and rejects ambiguous versions or overlapping
periods. Receipts reconcile to their cover summaries. Review annotations
cannot change filed amounts, employers, dates or committee IDs; missing or
changed reviewed dependencies stop the build. No name/amount deduplication
or generic addition of JFC allocations to the global bulk pool was introduced.

Attribution now separates direct individual receipts, reviewed JFC allocations,
supported partnership shares, signed adjustments and linked refunds. Unknown
receipt relationships, incomplete report coverage, unreviewed account ownership,
or multiple campaign accounts with unreconciled donor overlap withhold a combined
amount. Unknown refund identities or incomplete refund coverage withhold net.
Nonmemo receipts are not labeled universally as cash because in-kind receipts
can appear on those lines. Unexplained memos remain unresolved.
